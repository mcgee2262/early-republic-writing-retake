### Early Republic Writing Retake

A study guide for the US History short-answer retake. The teacher picks **5 of these 10 topics**, and you write a few sentences on each:

1. Articles of Confederation
2. Constitutional Compromises
3. Virginians at the Convention
4. Federalists vs. Anti-Federalists
5. George Washington
6. Interactions with Britain & France
7. Election of 1800
8. War of 1812: Causes & Outcomes
9. Missouri Compromise
10. Monroe Doctrine

**Inside the guide:**
- **Lessons** with slides read aloud by a natural voice (Andrew or Ava), ending with a full-credit answer for each topic
- **Flashcards** and a **Quiz Show** game
- **President Lineup**: drag the first five presidents into order
- **Practice Test**: write answers for 5 random topics and see which key ideas you hit

Your progress (check marks, scores, test answers) is saved in your own browser.
